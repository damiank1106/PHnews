1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Seismic Co. (“the App”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) accesses, collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the App.

The App is designed to provide seismic-related information, such as earthquake, tsunami, volcanic activity, alerts, maps, and related content. Depending on which features are enabled in your version of the App, certain information may be processed as described below.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information you provide directly

  • Account information, such as username, email address, or profile details, if you create an account.
  • Messages, reports, saved items, or other content you choose to submit in the App.
  • Support requests or emails you send to us.

B. Information collected automatically

  • Device information such as device model, operating system, app version, and language.
  • Basic usage information, such as screens viewed and app interactions.
  • Crash logs and diagnostic data, if crash reporting tools are enabled.

C. Location information

The App does not access your device location. If location is enabled, it is used only for features such as showing nearby seismic activity, centering maps, or location-based alerts.

D. Notifications

The App does use push notifications. If enabled, notifications may be used for seismic alerts, app updates, or important service messages.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide core app features and display seismic activity information.
  • To improve app performance, reliability, and user experience.
  • To maintain security and detect errors or misuse.
  • To respond to support questions and user requests.
  • To send notifications, if you choose to enable them.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share limited data only when necessary with service providers that support the App, such as hosting, database, analytics, crash reporting, notification, or map providers.

  • [Supabase] – if you use login, cloud sync, database, or storage features.
  • [Firebase / FCM] – if you use push notifications or analytics.
  • [Map provider] – if the App displays online maps or location layers.
  • Official data providers – if the App retrieves public seismic data from government or scientific sources.

We may also disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

5. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Some information may be stored locally on your device for faster loading, offline access, or saved preferences. If cloud sync is enabled, some information may also be stored on secure third-party infrastructure used by the App.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as needed to provide the App, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Locally stored data may remain on your device until you delete the App, clear app storage, or remove the content manually.

7. Your Choices

  • You may request deletion of your account or personal data by contacting us at the email below, if account features are available.
